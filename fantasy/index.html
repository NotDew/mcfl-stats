MSSN Fantasy League · Season 19

MCFL Fantasy

Make an account, then pick a league from the Leagues tab or create your own. Leagues can be public, or private with a password. Everyone drafts live, in snake order, and bots take any empty seats. Then run your team all season: set your lineup each week, pick up and drop players, claim off waivers and trade with the other managers.

  • Points come from the real MCFL games, as they're logged.
  • Be in several leagues with one account, and switch between them.
  • Join after the draft and you take over a bot's team.
  • Top 4 after Week 5 make the playoffs.

The league lives on the league server, so you can sign in from any device.

We work out every point ourselves from the game logs (the logs' own fantasy points aren't used). Players score only on offense, and a player is a QB or WR by what they do with the ball, so a completion is worth the same as a catch. Defense is scored for each real team as a whole (D/ST): it starts every game on its starting points, then gains for takeaways and sacks and loses for yards and TDs the other offense gets. The numbers are balanced on real MCFL stats, so a decent game at any spot is worth about the same and a great one is worth a lot more. A player's sub appearances don't count toward their points.
