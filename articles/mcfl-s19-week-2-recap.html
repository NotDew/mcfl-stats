Silverfish Shut Out the Totems 80-0 as the Raiders Crush the No. 2 Mountaineers

#MCFL#S19#Week 2#Power Rankings

Silverfish posted a second straight shutout behind 11 BaltimoreBrownie touchdown passes, the No. 7 Raiders beat the No. 2 Mountaineers 48-6, and the Devilbats scored 70.

Two games into Season 19, Silverfish have scored 143 points and allowed none. The follow-up to their 63-0 opener was an 80-0 win over the Totems in which BaltimoreBrownie threw 11 touchdown passes and the defense sacked the Totems 14 times. The Totems finished with 14 passing yards and -35 rushing yards.

The upset of the week went to the Raiders. Ranked seventh after a 66-37 opening loss, they went to the No. 2 Mountaineers and won 48-6. The Mountaineers, who threw 7 touchdown passes in week 1, managed one.

Winners

BaltimoreBrownie and Kofeyy. BaltimoreBrownie threw for 415 yards and 11 touchdowns without an interception, ran for 29 more, and added 2 sacks and an interception on defense for a league-high 189.0 fantasy points. Kofeyy caught 11 passes for 324 yards and 7 touchdowns, then added 4 sacks.

Raiders. Phizix_ threw for 469 yards and 6 touchdowns, ran one in, and picked off 2 passes. Alexd8011 caught 15 balls for 334 yards and 4 scores and had 3 sacks. A 42-point road win over the No. 2 team moves the Raiders up to No. 6.

Devilbats. 70 points, 616 passing yards and 9 touchdown passes against the Wardens, plus 6 interceptions on defense. deybtww was MVP and now leads all quarterbacks in the MSSN rankings. The Devilbats jump two spots to No. 2.

Tropics. A 37-25 win over the Allays in a game with 15 interceptions. Jere7iah has 7 defensive interceptions in two games, and the Tropics are 2-0 with the No. 2 defense.

Losers

Totems. Outscored 133-7 in two games. They were shut out this week, gave up 14 sacks, and rank last in every unit.

Mountaineers. One touchdown pass, 4 interceptions thrown and -20 rushing yards. They drop two spots to No. 4.

Sniffers. The final went in the books as Traders 2, Sniffers 0, even though Phbc threw for 387 yards and 5 touchdowns and was named MVP. The Sniffers are 0-2 and fall to No. 8.

Wardens. 133 points allowed in two games, and 13 interceptions thrown by FredIsLurking.
