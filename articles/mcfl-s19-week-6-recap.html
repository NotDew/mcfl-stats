Allays Hand Silverfish Their First Loss in a 42-38 Rivalry Week Thriller

#MCFL#S19#Week 6#Rivalry Week#Power Rankings#Predictions

The Allays picked off BakkaGhostKofeyy 6 times to beat Silverfish 42-38, leaving three teams tied at 5-1, while the Wardens scored 99 and three rivalry games ended in forfeits.

There are no unbeaten teams left in the MCFL. In the marquee game of Rivalry Week, the Allays went to Silverfish and won 42-38. Silverfish had scored 374 points in five straight wins. The Allays intercepted BakkaGhostKofeyy 6 times, and Raccnn threw for 482 yards and 6 touchdowns and made 9 tackles and 2 sacks. MoneyManiac2234 caught 5 touchdown passes and also picked off 3 passes.

That leaves Silverfish, the Devilbats and the Allays all at 5-1. The Devilbats got there without playing: the Tropics forfeited to them, one of three Rivalry Week forfeits along with Sniffers at Raiders and Mountaineers at Traders. The only other game on the field was a 99-6 rout. The Wardens intercepted the Totems 10 times and climbed two spots to No. 5.

Winners

Allays. A 4-point road win over the No. 1 team pulls them level with Silverfish and the Devilbats at 5-1. They rank third in all three units, and Raccnn is the top-ranked defender in the league, with 64 tackles, 8 sacks and 6 interceptions, as well as the No. 2 quarterback.

Wardens. 99 points, 10 interceptions and 7 sacks against the Totems. FredIsLurking threw for 338 yards and 9 touchdowns and ran for 3 more, the best fantasy line of the week at 177.4. HD_81 scored 8 times in all: 6 receiving, 1 rushing and 1 on defense. The Wardens are 3-3 and up two spots to No. 5, and their point differential swung from -60 to +33 in one night.

Devilbats. A forfeit win over the Tropics puts them at 5-1, level with the top two. Their 343 points are second only to Silverfish.

Losers

Silverfish. The perfect season is over. BakkaGhostKofeyy threw for 511 yards and 6 touchdowns but was intercepted 6 times. Silverfish keep the No. 1 spot, with a +288 differential and the top unit in all three categories, but they now share the best record with two other teams.

Totems. A 99-6 loss leaves them 0-6, the only winless team in the league. They threw 10 interceptions. Through six games they've allowed 461 points.

Tropics. They forfeited a Rivalry Week game against the No. 2 team and fall to No. 6 at 3-3. Their defense still ranks second.

Sniffers and Traders. Both forfeited their rivalry games, and both fall to 1-5.
