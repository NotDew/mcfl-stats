Silverfish Blank Wardens 63-0 as Week 1 Turns Into a Shootout

#MCFL#S19#Week 1#Power Rankings#Predictions

Silverfish pitched a 63-0 shutout, Tacoman5569 threw 7 touchdowns without a pick, and five teams leave opening week unbeaten.

Season 19 opened with defenses taking the week off, mostly. Five games produced 368 points, three teams cleared 60, and four of the five finals were decided by 29 or more. The one exception to the scoring spree was the most lopsided result of the week: Silverfish went into Wardens territory and won 63-0, with 6 sacks, 7 interceptions and 2 defensive touchdowns.

The other headline belongs to Tacoman5569. The Mountaineers quarterback threw for 522 yards and 7 touchdowns with zero interceptions, a 138.8 QBR, then picked off 5 passes on defense in a 53-7 rout of the Totems. His 182.6 fantasy points led the league by 13.

Winners

Silverfish. A 63-0 shutout is as loud as a first week gets. Gunnarsser (5 sacks, an interception and a defensive touchdown) was named MVP. BaltimoreBrownie was efficient at quarterback: 323 yards, 6 touchdowns, 1 interception and a 128.6 QBR. They start the season No. 1 in the MSSN power rankings.

Tacoman5569 and the Mountaineers. 522 passing yards, 7 touchdowns, no picks. The Mountaineers defense added 9 interceptions and allowed just 7 points. That's the No. 1 quarterback room and the No. 2 defense after one week.

JtownW. The Tropics quarterback threw 10 touchdown passes in a 62-21 win over the Traders, 7 of them to Jere7iah (12 catches, 287 yards). The Tropics defense added 8 interceptions of its own.

canboon2. Named MVP of the Devilbats' 66-37 win over the Raiders after doing everything: 4 receiving touchdowns and 162 receiving yards, plus 3 sacks. Quarterback deybtww chipped in 436 passing yards, 7 touchdowns and 75 rushing yards.

Losers

Wardens. No points, 7 interceptions thrown and a 13.4 QBR. They were sacked 6 times and finished with -45 rushing yards. They rank last in every unit ranking after week 1.

Totems. Ten interceptions thrown in a 53-7 loss. You can't win when you're handing the ball away that often.

Sniffers. They lost the week's only close game, 33-26 to the Allays. Eight interceptions thrown did the damage in a game where they were otherwise competitive. Raccnn's 640 passing yards on the other side didn't help.

Traders. Gave up 62 points and threw 8 interceptions. The 41-point loss to the Tropics has them No. 8 to start.
