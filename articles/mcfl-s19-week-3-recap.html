Silverfish Hang 97 on the Traders While the Devilbats and Tropics Fall

#MCFL#S19#Week 3#Power Rankings#Predictions

Silverfish scored 97 and picked off 10 passes to stay perfect, the Allays edged the Devilbats 42-41, and the Wardens stunned the No. 3 Tropics for their first win.

Silverfish are the only unbeaten team left in the MCFL, and they are not just winning. They went to the Traders and won 97-26, throwing 13 touchdown passes and intercepting 10. Through three games they have outscored opponents 240-26, a point differential of 214. No other team is better than plus 70.

Behind them, the top of the table caved in. The No. 2 Devilbats lost 42-41 to the Allays, and the No. 3 Tropics lost 34-32 to the Wardens, a team that had been outscored 133-28 in its first two games. Six teams now sit at 2-1, and the MSSN power rankings had to sort them out by margin.

Winners

Silverfish. 788 passing yards, 13 passing touchdowns, a rushing touchdown and 10 interceptions on defense. BaltimoreBrownie took MVP again and is now at 27 touchdown passes against 4 interceptions with a 134.1 QBR. Silverfish rank No. 1 in all three units.

Kofeyy. The Silverfish receiver has 38 catches, 1,061 yards and 20 touchdowns in three games. No other receiver in the top five has more than 12. He also has 9 interceptions on defense, enough to crack the top five defenders.

Wardens. The worst team of the first two weeks beat the No. 3 team. Proffic was named MVP, the Wardens threw 5 touchdown passes, ran for 98 yards and sacked the Tropics 4 times. They climb two spots to No. 7.

Raiders. A 49-12 win over the Totems with 10 sacks, 5 of them from Chocomilk_. empluzedflamingo was MVP with 12 catches for 242 yards, 2 receiving touchdowns, a touchdown pass and 4 sacks. Two straight blowout wins move the Raiders up three spots to No. 3.

Losers

Tropics. Unbeaten and ranked third coming in, beaten by the No. 9 team. They threw for only 353 yards and drop three spots to No. 6, the biggest fall of the week.

Devilbats. deybtww threw for 513 yards and 6 touchdowns and still lost. He was named MVP anyway, one of two MVPs this week to come from the losing side. The Devilbats hold No. 2 but are no longer unbeaten.

Sniffers. 0-3, and the three losses add up to 13 points. Burtec was MVP against the Mountaineers with 365 receiving yards, 3 touchdowns, 12 tackles and 2 interceptions, and the defense recorded 7 sacks. It still ended 28-24 the wrong way.

Totems. 0-3 and outscored 182-19. They were sacked 10 times and finished with -104 rushing yards.
