Devilbats Hang 93 on the Totems as Silverfish Roll to 4-0

#MCFL#S19#Week 4#Power Rankings#Predictions

The Devilbats shut out the Totems 93-0, Silverfish crushed the No. 3 Raiders 57-13 to stay perfect, and the Wardens beat the Traders 74-6.

Week 4 in the MCFL was all about margins. The Devilbats beat the Totems 93-0, the largest margin of the week. canboon2 caught 5 touchdowns and recorded 7 sacks, and deybtww threw 10 touchdown passes and ran for 175 yards. The Devilbats' defense held the Totems to 90 passing yards and -57 rushing yards and picked off 5 passes.

At the top, Silverfish went to the No. 3 Raiders and won 57-13. They're 4-0, the only unbeaten team in the league, with a +258 point differential and the No. 1 ranking in all three units. The Wardens added a 74-6 win over the Traders, and the Allays edged the Mountaineers 46-40 in the only one-score game of the week.

Winners

Devilbats. A 93-0 shutout keeps them at No. 2 at 3-1. They've scored 270 points with a +163 differential. MVP canboon2 caught 6 passes for 139 yards and 5 touchdowns and added 7 sacks and an interception, the week's best fantasy line at 207.7. The Devilbats ran for 203 yards as a team.

Silverfish. BakkaGhostKofeyy threw for 596 yards and 8 touchdowns, ran for 46 yards and intercepted 2 passes. Silverfish picked off the Raiders 5 times. Through four games they've outscored opponents 297-39. BakkaGhostKofeyy leads all ranked quarterbacks with 35 touchdown passes and a 136.7 QBR.

Wardens. A 74-6 win over the Traders brings them to 2-2. FredIsLurking threw for 328 yards and 7 touchdowns and ran for 116 yards and 2 more scores. The defense recorded 10 sacks and 4 interceptions and held the Traders to -111 rushing yards. Gunnarsser was named MVP.

Allays. A 46-40 win over the Mountaineers moves them up to No. 3. MVP Raccnn threw for 615 yards and 6 touchdowns and made 26 tackles, and the Allays had 45 tackles and 7 sacks as a team. Raccnn is now the top-ranked defender in the league and the No. 3 quarterback.

Losers

Totems. They're 0-4 after a 93-0 loss. Through four games they've scored 19 points and allowed 275. They rank last in the power rankings and in all three units.

Traders. They managed 31 receiving yards and -111 rushing yards in a 74-6 loss. They're 1-3, have allowed 233 points and drop to No. 9 with a -178 differential.

Raiders. They came in at No. 3 and leave at No. 6, the biggest fall of the week. Five interceptions and 2 touchdown passes against Silverfish turned a top-three matchup into a 44-point loss. They're 2-2.

Sniffers. They lost by forfeit to the Tropics, 2-0, and are 0-4. Their defense still ranks third in the league, which is why they're No. 8 rather than at the bottom.
