Each tracked CB and MLB receives one -2.0 to +2.0 grade on every recorded pass snap. Route separation starts when a defender first gets within 5 yards of their assigned receiver. Each receiver is assigned to one closest defender; untargeted coverage deductions are capped at -0.5, below swat and interception value. Overall grade uses a custom transparent conversion: 50 + average snap grade × 25; this is not PFF's proprietary formula. DL are not graded as coverage defenders.
