Ghasts Survive 63-62 Shootout as Blazes Take Over No. 1

#MFL#S21#Week 3#Power Rankings#Predictions

The Ghasts outlasted the Magmas by a single point in a 125-point classic, the Blazes moved to 3-0 and the top of the MSSN power rankings, and a forfeit cost the Axolotls the No. 1 spot.

Week 3 produced the best game of the MFL season so far. The Ghasts and Magmas combined for 125 points, and the Ghasts won 63-62. The Ghasts threw for 833 yards. Cxozy caught 23 passes for 543 yards and 6 touchdowns, and Avoidss threw for 391 yards and 4 touchdowns and caught 2 more. On the other side, beefourr was the MVP in a losing effort: 601 passing yards, 8 touchdowns, 14 tackles and 3 interceptions on defense. The Ghasts are 3-0.

The other headline came without a snap. The Axolotls, No. 1 coming into the week, forfeited to the Breeze, a 2-0 result. That let the Blazes take over the top spot after a 47-28 win over the Phantoms. The Blazes and Ghasts are now the only unbeaten teams in the league.

Winners

Blazes. At 3-0 and No. 1 in the MSSN power rankings. Against the Phantoms they had 6 sacks and 5 interceptions and threw just 1 pick themselves. Their defense now ranks first in the league. MVP 1e36 had 11 catches for 276 yards and 5 touchdowns, plus 3 sacks, 1 interception and 8 tackles. Vx3sty threw for 471 yards and 7 touchdowns.

Ghasts. It took a one-point game to get there, but the Ghasts are 3-0 and hold onto No. 3. Their receiving corps is now ranked first in the league. Cxozy leads all receivers with 48 catches and 967 yards on the season.

Gators. First win of the season, 54-41 over the Dragons, and the biggest climb of the week: up two spots to No. 8. MVP Dead_Rr threw for 576 yards and 8 touchdowns. B0TZ_ is now the top-ranked receiver in the league with 961 yards and 13 touchdowns.

Guardians. A 50-28 win over the Polar Bears moves them up to No. 4 at 2-1. Their 162 points are the most in the league. MVP slicksters now has 903 receiving yards in two games, and BradTheCool leads all quarterbacks with 1,777 yards.

Losers

Axolotls. A forfeit loss to the Breeze cost them the No. 1 spot. On the field they're still elite: they have the league's best point differential (+86) and its top-ranked quarterback room. At 2-1, they fall to No. 2.

Magmas. Scoring 62 points was not enough. beefourr's 601 yards and 8 touchdowns came with 5 interceptions, and the Magmas are 1-2 despite a +17 point differential. They slip to No. 5.

Polar Bears. At 0-3 with a -88 point differential, they are last in the power rankings. They also rank last at quarterback and receiver. They threw 5 interceptions in the 50-28 loss to the Guardians.

Phantoms. Their defense ranks second in the league, but it allowed 47 points to the Blazes, and the offense threw 5 interceptions. At 1-2 they drop to No. 7.
