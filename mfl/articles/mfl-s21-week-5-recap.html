Gators Stun the Blazes 44-42 as Ghasts Stay Perfect

#MFL#S21#Week 5#Power Rankings#Predictions

The Gators knocked off the No. 2 Blazes by two points, the Ghasts intercepted the Axolotls 7 times to move to 5-0, and BradTheCool's 637 yards lifted the Guardians to No. 2.

The upset of the week came from the No. 7 Gators, who beat the No. 2 Blazes 44-42. nood threw for 637 yards and 4 touchdowns, and B0TZ_ caught 11 passes for 386 yards and 4 scores, ran for another, and intercepted 3 passes on defense. The Blazes' 1e36 was named MVP in the loss with 19 catches, 408 yards and 5 touchdowns plus a rushing score. It's the Blazes' second straight loss, and the two have come by a combined 3 points.

The Ghasts remain the only unbeaten team. They beat the No. 3 Axolotls 47-31 behind a defense that intercepted 7 passes and recorded 5 sacks, with DragonBallZ named MVP. The Guardians beat the Magmas 55-49 and the Breeze beat the Phantoms 36-24.

Winners

Gators. A two-point win over the No. 2 team moves them to 3-2. They're still No. 7 in the MSSN power rankings because of a -24 point differential, but B0TZ_ is now the No. 3 receiver in the league with 19 touchdowns.

Ghasts. At 5-0, they're No. 1 for the second straight week, and their defense is now ranked first in the league. DragonBallZ leads all defenders with 12 sacks and a defensive touchdown, and Avoidss is No. 4 with 10 interceptions.

Guardians. A 55-49 win over the Magmas puts them at 4-1 and up two spots to No. 2. BradTheCool threw for 637 yards and 7 touchdowns, caught a touchdown pass, and intercepted 3 passes on defense, for 175.5 fantasy points, the most of the week. The Guardians' 271 points are the most in the league.

Breeze. A 36-24 win over the Phantoms, with L8_Knight named MVP, moves them up two spots to No. 6 at 3-2.

Losers

Blazes. Two losses in two weeks, by 1 and 2 points. They fall to No. 3 at 3-2. Their +60 point differential is still second-best in the league, and their defense ranks second.

Axolotls. Seven interceptions thrown and -67 rushing yards in a 16-point loss to the Ghasts. At 2-3 they drop to No. 4, though their +68 differential is still the best in the league.

Magmas. They scored 49 points and still lost. beefourr threw for 571 yards and 6 touchdowns but was intercepted 5 times. Cozyyy had 14 catches, 262 yards, 3 touchdowns, 13 tackles and 2 interceptions. The Magmas are 2-3 at No. 5.

Phantoms. A 12-point loss to the Breeze drops them two spots to No. 8 at 2-3. Their defense still ranks third.
