Guardians Go From Last to Fifth With a 77-33 Rout of the Breeze

#MFL#S21#Week 2#Power Rankings#Predictions

The No. 10 Guardians picked off the Breeze 8 times in a 44-point upset, BigPoppaRS had 474 receiving yards and 5 interceptions, and the Blazes and Ghasts moved to 2-0.

A week after giving up 74 points, the Guardians handed out the biggest beating of the young season. They came into Week 2 ranked last in the MSSN power rankings, travelled to face the No. 4 Breeze, and won 77-33. The 44-point margin is the largest of the season, and it was the only upset of the week.

Three of the five games were played. The Blazes beat the Magmas 50-32 in a matchup of top-three teams, and the Ghasts held off the Gators 34-27 in the closest game of the week. Axolotls at Polar Bears and Phantoms at Dragons were not played. That left the Axolotls at 1-0 and still No. 1, with the Blazes and Ghasts now the only 2-0 teams.

Winners

Guardians. In Week 1 they threw 7 interceptions. In Week 2 their defense took 8 off the Breeze, and the offense threw for 696 yards and 10 touchdowns. That's a jump of five spots to No. 5. The Guardians now rank third among quarterback rooms and second among receiving corps.

BigPoppaRS. The Guardians' MVP had the best game of the season so far: 20 catches for 474 yards and 8 touchdowns, plus a rushing touchdown, a touchdown pass and 5 interceptions on defense. That's 186.9 fantasy points, the most this week by almost 20. BradTheCool added 637 passing yards and 9 touchdowns, good for No. 1 in the quarterback rankings with 1,252 yards on the season.

Blazes. A clean 50-32 win over the No. 2 Magmas. The Blazes sacked the Magmas 7 times, ran for 2 touchdowns and threw only 1 interception. MVP Vx3sty threw for 393 yards and 5 touchdowns and made 10 tackles. At 2-0 and +44, the Blazes move up to No. 2, and their defense ranks second in the league.

Ghasts. Two wins and a two-spot climb to No. 3. Avoidss did it all against the Gators: 524 passing yards, 5 touchdowns, 72 rushing yards and 10 tackles. Avoidss is also the top-ranked defender in the league, and teammate DragonBallZ is second.

Losers

Breeze. A 30-13 Week 1 win looks a long way off now. The Breeze threw 8 interceptions, gave up 77 points and fell two spots to No. 6. Their point differential dropped from +17 to -27. 1of1___ did their part with 12 catches, 363 yards, 3 touchdowns and 13 tackles, and Robby had 4 sacks, but the turnovers decided the game.

Magmas. They came in at No. 2 and leave at No. 4. The offense moved the ball (522 passing yards) but managed only 4 touchdowns and didn't record a sack. beefourr still ranks second among quarterbacks and third among defenders, which is why the Magmas remain in the top half.

Gators. At 0-2 they are the only team with two losses. They got as close as 7 points, but 4 interceptions and -78 rushing yards against the Ghasts were too much to overcome. Dead_Rr has thrown 10 interceptions in two games, the most of any ranked quarterback. They slip to No. 9.

Dragons. They didn't play this week and still fell to No. 10. They rank last in all three units: quarterbacks, receivers and defense.
